About

Data is only useful when people can trust it. Making sure they can is what I do.

I'm a Senior Data Operations Analyst at NielsenIQ in Chennai, part of the team behind the retail and consumer data that brands and retailers use to make big decisions. Since July 2022 I've worked hands-on with large datasets every single day: validating them, catching problems early, tracing issues back to the root cause and fixing them before they ever reach a client.

Finance mindset

One thing I've shown again and again is that I learn fast. New tool, new process, new kind of data — I pick it up and get it working. Next, I want to go deeper into analytics, business intelligence and data-driven decision making.
